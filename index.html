01 · The rule

Your part stays real.

AI is great at making worlds and terrible at making your product. Let it draw a brake lever and you get the wrong shape, a melted logo, a chain on the wrong side. Riders spot it in a second.

So AI never draws your part. We take it from your own photos and TikToks, cut it out cleanly, and check every frame against the real thing. AI only builds the world around it: frost, zero gravity, a desert at midnight.

08 · Questions

Is it AI? Will it look fake?

The world around your product is AI-generated. Your product never is: it comes from your own photos and footage, checked frame by frame. We label AI content the way TikTok and Meta require.

Will my part look exactly right?

Yes, or it doesn't ship. Anything that warps, from a logo to a bolt, gets redone.

Do I get 15 videos on Starter?

No. Starter is 15 postable creatives with up to 5 videos; the rest are statics and variants.

What do you need from me?

Photos of your part on its own and installed on a bike, your logo, and the bikes it fits. Phone photos are fine.

Why pay when TikTok and Meta have free AI tools?

Use them for quick variants. They won't come up with a concept for your bike, check that your part came out right, or remake what won last month. That takes someone's time. We're that time.

Do you show results?

Our first tests are running now. We'll post real numbers when we have them, not before.
