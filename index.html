Questions you'll have

See all questions

Is it AI? Will it look fake?

The scene around your product can be AI. Your product isn't: it comes from your own photos, and a person checks every file. We label AI content the way TikTok and Meta require.

When do I pay?

Not when you apply. If we accept you, we email a payment link. Your first month starts when you pay it.

Can I cancel?

Nothing renews on its own. Each month we email a new payment link. If you don't pay it, your plan stops. No contract.

Do I get 15 videos on Starter?

No. Starter is 15 creatives a month, and up to 5 of them can be videos. The rest are statics and variations.

Do you run my ads?

No. We make the ads. You or your ad person run them, and you pay TikTok or Meta directly.
