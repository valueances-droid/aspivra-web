The ads

Is it AI? Will it look fake?

The scene around your product can be AI. Your product isn't: it comes from your own photos, and a person checks every file. We label AI content the way TikTok and Meta require.

Will my part look exactly right?

That's the whole point. If anything on your product is off, from a logo to a bolt, we redo it before it ships.

What kinds of ads do you make?

Short vertical videos and static ads: unboxings, before and after, your bike somewhere it shouldn't be, parts floating into place, and launch reveals on Scale.

Will ads claim top speed, range or street legality?

Only what's on your own site. We use your published specs and nothing else.

Your product

What do you need from me?

3 to 5 photos of each product you want ads for, a few on the bike, your logo, and which bikes it fits. Phone photos work.

I don't have good photos.

Send links to your own TikToks. We can pull clean frames from them.

Do you work with bike brands or just parts shops?

Both. Parts, decals, gear and bikes, as long as it's e-moto.

Plans and billing

Do I get 15 videos on Starter?

No. Starter is 15 creatives a month, and up to 5 of them can be videos. The rest are statics and variations.

What counts as a creative?

One finished file you can post: a video, a static, or a variation of one with a new hook or scene. Simple resizes don't count.

When do I pay?

Not when you apply. If we accept you, we email a payment link. Your first month starts when you pay it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Nothing renews on its own. Each month we email a new payment link. If you don't pay it, your plan stops. No contract.

Is ad spend included?

No. You run the ads and pay TikTok or Meta directly.

Can I add more videos?

Yes. Extra videos are $15 each, on top of your plan.

Working together

How fast is the first batch?

5 to 7 business days after we have your payment and photos.

Do you run my ads?

No. We make the ads. You or your ad person run them.

Why pay when TikTok and Meta have free AI tools?

Use them for quick variations. They won't come up with a concept for your bike, check that your part came out right, or remake what did well last month. That takes a person's time. That's what you're paying for.

Why do I have to apply?

We only take shops we can do good work for: e-moto brands, and products we can cut cleanly from your photos. If we can't, we'll tell you why. You don't pay anything either way.

Do you show results?

Our first tests are in progress. We'll post real numbers when we have them, not before.
