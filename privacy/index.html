Privacy

Short version: we only use what you send us to review your application and talk to you about it.

What we collect

When you apply, we get what you type into the form: your name, email, brand or store name, store website or social link, what you sell, where you run ads, the plan you picked, and your social handle if you add one.

Where it goes

Your application is saved to a private Google Sheet in our team's Google Drive, and we get an email at aspivrallc@gmail.com letting us know it arrived. Only Gabo, Tyler and the AI tools we use to sort applications read it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they see your IP address when you visit. We don't run analytics or ad trackers.

Why we use it

To review your application, reply to you, and, if we work together, to make your ads.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't share it with anyone outside Aspivra, apart from the services named above.

Your photos and files

If we work together, we use the photos and logos you send only to make your ads.

Deleting your data

Email aspivrallc@gmail.com and we'll delete your application.
