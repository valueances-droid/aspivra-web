Terms

The plain version of how working with us works.

Who we are

Aspivra LLC makes video and static ads for e-moto brands and parts shops. Contact: aspivrallc@gmail.com.

Applying

Applying is free and doesn't commit you to anything. We review applications and reply within a week. We may not be able to take every brand.

Plans and payment

Plans are month to month, with no contract. If we accept you, we email your plan details and a payment link. Your first month starts when you pay it. Nothing is charged before that. Nothing renews on its own: each month we email a new payment link, and if you don't pay it, your plan stops. The exact terms for your plan are confirmed in writing before you pay.

What a creative is

One finished file you can post: a video, a static ad, or a variation of one with a new hook or scene. Videos are part of your plan's total, not on top of it. Extra videos are $15 each, on top of your plan.

Your content

You confirm you have the rights to the photos, videos and logos you send us. We use them only to make your ads.

Accuracy

Your product comes from your own photos, never AI-drawn. If your part comes out wrong in a file we deliver, we redo it.

AI content

Scenes around your product can be AI-generated. We label AI content the way each platform requires.

What we don't do

We don't run your ad accounts or pay for ad spend, and we don't promise sales, views or results.
