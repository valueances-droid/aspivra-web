How paying works

No card to apply. No charge until we've said yes and you've seen your plan details.

  1. Apply for a plan

    Free, about two minutes.

  2. We review it

    We reply within a week from aspivrallc@gmail.com.

  3. Pay the link we send

    If you're in, we email a payment link for your first month. We never ask for card details by email.

  4. Send photos, get ads

    Your first batch lands 5 to 7 business days after your payment and photos. Your next link comes a month after you pay.

Every plan includes

  • Your real product, cut from your photos. Never AI-drawn.
  • A person checks every file before it ships. If your part comes out wrong, we redo it.
  • Files sized for TikTok, Reels and Meta.
  • AI content labeled the way each platform requires.
  • First batch 5 to 7 business days after your payment and photos.

What counts as a creative

A creative is one finished file you can post: a video, a static ad, or a variation of one with a new hook or a new scene.

Videos are part of the total, not on top of it. On Starter, that means up to 5 videos and the rest statics and variations, 15 in all.

Simple resizes of the same ad don't use up a creative. Fixing a mistake we made never does.

Pricing questions

See all questions

When am I charged?

Not when you apply. If we accept you, we email a payment link. Your plan starts when you pay it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Nothing renews on its own. Each month we email a new payment link. If you don't pay it, your plan stops. No contract.

Do I get 15 videos on Starter?

No. Starter is 15 creatives a month, and up to 5 of them can be videos. The rest are statics and variations.

Why is it this cheap?

AI builds the scenes, so there's no studio, no crew and no actors. We only do e-moto, so we already know the bikes and parts. The formats are set, and we don't run your ad account. A person still checks every file against your real product.

Is ad spend included?

No. You run the ads and pay TikTok or Meta directly. We make the ads.

What if my part comes out wrong?

We redo it. If it doesn't match your photos, it doesn't ship.

Do you promise sales or views?

No. Our first tests are in progress. We make ads for you to test, and we'll share real numbers when we have them.
