How it works

You send photos. We send back a month of ads. Here's every step.

  1. Apply

    Tell us what you sell and which plan you want. Two minutes. Nothing to pay.

  2. We review it

    We read every application and reply within a week from aspivrallc@gmail.com.

  3. Pay and send photos

    If we're a fit, we email a payment link for your plan. We never ask for card details by email. Pay it, then send 3 to 5 photos of each product you want ads for, plus a few on the bike. Phone photos are fine.

  4. First batch

    5 to 7 business days after we have your payment and photos.

  5. Every month after

    A new batch each month. On Growth and Scale, tell us which ads did best and we make new versions with fresh hooks and scenes.

Your product is never redrawn

AI is good at building worlds and bad at drawing your product. Ask it for a brake lever and you get the wrong shape and a melted logo. So we don't ask.

What we need from you

  • 3 to 5 photos of each product you want ads for
  • A few photos of it on the bike
  • Your logo
  • Which bikes it fits
  • Anything you're launching soon

No good photos? Send links to your own TikToks. We can pull clean frames from them.

What you get

  • Short vertical videos for TikTok and Reels
  • Static ads for Meta feed and stories
  • A hook on screen in the first second
  • Your site or handle at the end
  • Files you can upload straight to TikTok, Reels or Meta Ads Manager

The formats

Built from what's getting views for e-moto brands on TikTok right now.

It just arrived
POV unboxing on the bench, then the bike lands somewhere it shouldn't.
Stock vs upgraded
Before and after in one cut. The scene changes with the part.
Wrong place, right bike
Your bike on a kitchen counter, at a gas pump at midnight, on the moon.
Floating parts
The kit hangs in the air and snaps onto the frame.
New drop
A reveal for a new part or bike, built for launch week. On Scale.

What we don't do

  • We don't run your ad account. We make the ads, you run them.
  • We don't invent bikes or parts that don't exist.
  • We don't claim top speed, range or street legality beyond what's on your own site.
  • We don't promise sales. Our first tests are in progress, and we'll share real numbers when we have them.
